为加速 BEV感知模型从研发到验证的闭环，Apollo 自动驾驶平台提供了一套开箱即用的工具链：

**模型训练**[Apollo-BEV-Train](https://apollo.baidu.com/docs/apollo/latest/md_docs_2_xE5_xBA_x94_xE7_x94_xA8_xE5_xAE_x9E_xE8_xB7_xB5_2_xE5_xBC_x80_xE5_x8F_x91_xE8_xB0_x83_bfebeccbf6a24fc78a8c643019f79d65.html)：<https://console.bce.baidu.com/aihc/quickStart/detail?templateId=3928100>

**模型导出**[Apollo-BEV-Model-Export](https://apollo.baidu.com/docs/apollo/latest/md_docs_2_xE5_xBA_x94_xE7_x94_xA8_xE5_xAE_x9E_xE8_xB7_xB5_2_xE5_xBC_x80_xE5_x8F_x91_xE8_xB0_x83_337ccf708ac2a55dcec9e39fa5057a2d.html)：<https://console.bce.baidu.com/aihc/quickStart/detail?templateId=5599233>

**模型验证**[Apollo-Model-Deployment](https://apollo.baidu.com/docs/apollo/latest/md_docs_2_xE5_xBA_x94_xE7_x94_xA8_xE5_xAE_x9E_xE8_xB7_xB5_2_xE5_xBC_x80_xE5_x8F_x91_xE8_xB0_x83_22a3528eb837316534971b35532d13bb.html)：<https://console.bce.baidu.com/aihc/quickStart/detail?templateId=9770074>

用户可按此顺序依次使用三个标准化环境，高效完成 BEV 模型的开发与评估。
